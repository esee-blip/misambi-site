What guides us

Our values

Compassion

We treat every patient the way we would want our own family to be treated.

Dignity

Care is given with respect, privacy and without judgement, whatever a person's circumstances.

Integrity

We are honest with our patients and accountable to our donors for every resource entrusted to us.

Community

We work alongside local leaders, health workers and volunteers, because lasting change is shared.
