Volunteer

Give a day, change a life

Our camps run on the generosity of volunteers. You don't need to be medical to make a real difference.

Health professionals

  • Doctors, clinical officers and nurses
  • Pharmacists and pharmaceutical technologists
  • Lab technologists, dentists and optometrists

Everyone else

  • Patient registration, crowd flow and translation
  • Logistics, setup, photography and social media
  • Community mobilisation before each camp

Frequently asked questions

[Yes. State your registration type and number, e.g. "We are registered as a ... under ..., registration number ..."]

How are donations used?

Donations pay for medicines, medical supplies, lab tests, transport and meals for volunteers at our camps. [Add how you report back to donors, e.g. annual report or updates after each camp.]

Can I get a receipt for my donation?

Yes. Email info@misambimedicalmission.org with your name, amount and M-Pesa or bank reference, and we'll send you a receipt.

Do I need medical training to volunteer?

No. Many of our most important roles, like registration, logistics and community mobilisation, need no medical background, just a willing heart.

Can you bring a medical camp to my community?

We'd love to hear from you. Send us a request with the location and a local contact, and we'll get back to you about possible dates.

Have a question before you give?

We're happy to talk about our work, our finances or how you can help.
